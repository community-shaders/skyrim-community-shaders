# Community Shaders Trademark Policy

This policy explains how the "Community Shaders" name and logo may be used.

The code in this repository is free software under the GPL-3.0 with the
exceptions listed in [EXCEPTIONS.md](EXCEPTIONS.md). **Nothing in this policy
limits your rights under that licence.** You may use, modify and redistribute
the code as the licence allows. This policy covers only the project's names
and branding, which are not licensed under the GPL. That position is permitted
by section 7(e) of the GPL-3.0, which allows a project to decline to grant
rights under trademark law for some names, trademarks or service marks.

## What this policy covers

- **Name:** "Community Shaders" and "Skyrim Community Shaders".
- **Logo:** the Community Shaders logo and related brand artwork in
  [`package/Interface/CommunityShaders/Icons/Community Shaders Logo/`](package/Interface/CommunityShaders/Icons/Community%20Shaders%20Logo/).
  The logo is also subject to the terms in the [README](README.md#icons).

These are referred to below as "the marks". They belong to the Community
Shaders team, which controls how they are used.

## Uses that do not need permission

You may use the name, without asking, to describe the software accurately.
For example:

- Stating that your mod, preset or guide requires, works with or is
  compatible with Community Shaders.
- Describing a fork honestly, such as "a fork of Community Shaders".
- Writing about, reviewing, linking to or discussing Community Shaders.
- Building the software from source for your own use, including the logo
  that ships in the source tree, unmodified and for personal use only.

In all of these, use the name only as much as needed to identify the
software, and do not suggest that your project is official or endorsed.

## Uses that require permission

Ask first before you:

- Use the marks in the name of any project, product, mod, service, company,
  organisation, domain name or social media handle (for example
  "Community Shaders Plus" or `communityshaders-downloads.com`).
- Use the logo anywhere other than as allowed above, or modify it, or fold it
  into your own logo or branding.
- Redistribute, mirror or host Community Shaders builds (modified or not)
  under the Community Shaders name or logo, or with the logo and other brand
  artwork included. The GPL lets anyone redistribute the code, but not the
  branding. Without permission, remove the logo and brand artwork, do not
  present the copy as the official release, and identify it plainly as an
  unofficial copy.
- Distribute a **modified** build or fork under the Community Shaders name or
  logo. Forks are welcome under the GPL, but must be released under a
  different name and logo unless the team has agreed otherwise.
- Use the marks in advertising, sponsored listings or paid promotion.
- Use the marks in any way that implies affiliation with, or endorsement by,
  the Community Shaders team.
- Use the marks commercially, including on pages that sell or gate downloads,
  or that monetise access to the software.

## Mod hosting sites, aggregators and mirrors

If you host or list Community Shaders on a site you run, you need permission
to use the name or logo for the listing (see above). Without it:

- Do not present the listing as official, or as uploaded or approved by the
  team, unless it was.
- Do not use the logo or other brand artwork, and do not distribute builds
  that include them.
- Keep the GPL licence text and credits with the files, and make the source
  available as the licence requires.
- Link to the official download pages so users can find the maintained
  version.

Listings that break this policy may be reported to the hosting site, its
host or its registrar. The team may send copyright notices for the logo and
brand artwork, and trademark complaints for misuse of the name.

## Names for forks and derived projects

Pick a name that shows your project is separate from the original. You may
say it is based on or compatible with Community Shaders. Do not use a name that
could be confused with the official project, such as one that adds "Official",
"Pro" or "New" to the name, or that copies its logo's look.

## Asking for permission

To ask for permission, or to report a misuse, contact the Community Shaders
team on the [project Discord](https://discord.com/invite/nkrQybAsyy) or open
an issue in this repository. Say what you want to do, where the marks would
appear and whether the use is commercial.

Permission is granted at the team's discretion. It is more likely for
non-commercial projects whose main purpose is to support or improve
Community Shaders, and less likely for projects tied to people or
organisations that do not comply with the GPL.

## Changes to this policy

The team may update this policy. The version in the `dev` branch is the
current one. Questions about licensing of the code are answered by
[COPYING.txt](COPYING.txt) and [EXCEPTIONS.md](EXCEPTIONS.md), not by this
document.
